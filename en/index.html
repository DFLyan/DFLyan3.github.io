Biography

Jiwei Chen is a Ph.D. candidate in Computer and Information Engineering at the School of Science and Engineering, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Rui Huang. He received his M.Eng. in Control Science and Engineering from Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology (NUIST), advised by Prof. Yubao Sun, and his B.Eng. in Automation from NUIST.

His research interests include compressed sensing image reconstruction, 3D object detection for autonomous driving, and vision-language-action (VLA) models for driving. His work has been published in Pattern Recognition, IEEE TIP, IEEE TMM, ECCV, and IROS, and he holds several authorized and published patents. His current work focuses on open-world 3D object detection for open roads, and on detecting abnormal-condition objects and understanding scene semantics in driving scenarios.

Research Interests

Compressed Sensing Image Reconstruction

Deep-network-based CS image reconstruction, including generative adversarial networks, dual-path attention, memory-augmented cascading, and non-local regularization methods.

3D Object Detection for Autonomous Driving

BEV-based 3D object detection, and VLM-based open-world 3D detection for 3D localization and open-vocabulary semantic description of known and unknown objects.

Autonomous Driving VLA Models

Architecture, training, and closed-loop validation of vision-language-action models for driving, covering data annotation loops, training acceleration, simulation-based validation, and reinforcement learning.
